FAQ

How is age calculated?

We count full years, then full months, then remaining days between your date of birth and the comparison date — the same way you'd count it by hand on a calendar.

Is my birth date stored anywhere?

No. The calculation runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript — nothing is sent to a server or saved.

Can I calculate age on a future or past date?

Yes — change "Calculate age as of" to any date to see age at that point, useful for eligibility rules, legal ages, or planning ahead.
