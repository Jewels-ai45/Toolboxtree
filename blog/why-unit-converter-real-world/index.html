How to convert units instantly, without the mental math

Converting between units by hand is one of those small tasks that's easy to get slightly wrong under pressure — especially mid-recipe or mid-DIY project. ToolboxTree's free Unit Converter handles length, weight, temperature and volume instantly, right in your browser.

Convert something now

Pick a category, type a number, and see every common unit update instantly.

Open Unit Converter

Pick a category, then type

Start by choosing what you're converting — length, weight, temperature, or volume — then type a number into any field. Every other unit in that category updates at the same time, so you don't need to pick a "from" and "to" unit separately or hunt for a specific conversion pair.

Switch direction any time

Because every field updates together, there's no separate "reverse" button to look for — just type into whichever unit you actually have the number for, whether that's the one you're converting from or the one you're converting to.

When precision matters

For everyday use — cooking, travel, rough estimates — the default precision is more than enough. If you're working on something where an exact figure matters, like a technical drawing or a recipe you're scaling for a large batch, it's worth double-checking the rounded figure against the full decimal value before relying on it.
