About ToolboxTree

ToolboxTree is a small collection of free, focused tools — calculators, PDF and image tools, converters, and a planner — built to do one job well and get out of your way.

No accounts, no forced newsletter pop-ups, no ten-step wizard before you get to the thing you actually came for. Open a tool, use it, done.

How the tools work

Every tool runs client-side, directly in your browser. When you split a PDF, resize an image, or run a calculation, that work happens on your device — the file or text you're working with is never uploaded to a server unless a tool explicitly says otherwise. Close the tab and it's gone; nothing lingers on our end.

That also means the tools are fast — there's no upload/download round trip to wait on.

Why free?

Most tools here have no sign-up and no paywall. The site is supported by light, unobtrusive advertising and by an optional paid upgrade to the Planner, so the core tools can stay free for everyone.

Who's behind it

ToolboxTree is an independent, one-person project — built by someone who got tired of tool sites that bury a simple calculator under sign-up walls, autoplay ads and dark patterns, and decided to build the version that doesn't do that. New tools and improvements ship regularly based on what people actually use and ask for.
