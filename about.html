About ToolboxTree

ToolboxTree is a small collection of free, focused tools — calculators, PDF and image tools, converters, and a planner — built to do one job well and get out of your way.

Every tool runs in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded or stored on a server unless a tool specifically says so.

Why free?

Most tools here have no sign-up and no paywall. The site is supported by light, unobtrusive advertising and by the optional Planner upgrade, so the core tools can stay free.
