Free PDF Tools

Merge, split and compress PDFs — everything runs locally in your browser. Your files are never uploaded anywhere.

Merge PDFs

Choose two or more PDFs — they'll be combined in the order you select them.

Split / extract pages

Choose a PDF, then pick the page range you want pulled out into a new file.

Compress a PDF

Strips unused objects and re-packs the file structure. Works best on text-heavy or annotated PDFs — savings on scanned/image-heavy PDFs will be smaller.

FAQ

Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

No. Every operation — merging, splitting and compressing — runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your files never leave your device.
